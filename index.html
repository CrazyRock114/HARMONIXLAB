📖 Harmonix Lab Field Guide

Welcome to Harmonix Lab! This platform was built from first physical and mathematical principles to make music theory deeply intuitive through interactive play.

  • Zero Audio Samples: All instruments (guzheng, flute, violin, rhodes, analog synths, and drums) are synthesized in real-time in pure code using the Web Audio API and Karplus-Strong physical modeling!

  • Tap Hero Game: Use keys A and L (or click the pads) to train your brain on polyrhythms.
